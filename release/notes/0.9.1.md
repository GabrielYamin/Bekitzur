- AI apps like Claude, Claude Code, and Cursor can now work with your Conversations: find them, read their Notes and Transcripts, and change their Notes, with Undo in the Conversation's Chat tab. It's off until you turn on Settings → AI apps → Let AI apps use your Conversations. What an AI app reads goes to its model's provider, like Anthropic or OpenAI.
- In the same pane, Copy setup copies the entry to paste into an AI app's MCP servers. If an AI app can't add MCP servers, Add the skill lets Cursor's or Claude Code's agent run the same tools from its terminal.
- Settings is now split into panes with a sidebar, like System Settings, instead of one long list.
