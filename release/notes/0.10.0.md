- A new look: Record is in the toolbar, Conversations are grouped by day with who was in them, and the Notes read like a document.
- While recording, write your own notes beside what's being said. The Notes add what your notes say, like a task nobody said aloud, and show them in the margin, where you can also add or change them afterwards.
- Under the Transcript, a timeline shows who spoke when and where each item was said. The Transcript can also play along as lyrics.
- On a call, a small pill shows the time and both sound levels, with a pen for a quick note (⌥⌘N from any app) and Stop.
- If your microphone stops sending sound, Bekitzur tells you and lets you switch to another one.
