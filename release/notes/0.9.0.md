- Bekitzur is now in Hebrew too: Settings → Language picks English, Hebrew, or the Mac's language. In Hebrew, the whole app is translated and laid out right to left, and it's called בקיצור.
- Notes can be written in Hebrew, whatever language the Conversation is in: Settings → Language → Notes language. English stays the default, and Notes already written keep their language.
- An Action Item's due date now shows the time of day that was said, like "Thu, 12 Nov at 16:00", and its calendar event starts then.
- A Source under a Notes item or a chat answer now plays that moment in a small popup, instead of taking you to the Transcript.
- Ask is now Chat (beta). Besides answering from the Transcript and the Notes, it changes the Notes when you ask: add, change, or remove Decisions, Action Items, and Open Questions, check items off, rename, or shorten the summary. Each change shows in the chat with Undo. The chat is kept with its Conversation and works with Conversations up to 2 hours long.
- The chat can also use another Conversation's Notes: name it, like "my meeting with Dana last week", or pick it from the menu beside the message box. It finds Conversations by date and by the people you've named in Speakers.
