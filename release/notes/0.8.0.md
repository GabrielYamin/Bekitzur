- When two people talk at once, in a room or on the same side of a call, Bekitzur now separates their voices and writes down what each of them said. The Transcript shows their lines together, under "2 people at once". This needs a new 113 MB model: choose Download when Bekitzur offers it. It runs after the Notes are written, so they're ready as soon as before, and the Transcript fills in a few minutes later.
- On calls, sounds your microphone picks up while you're not speaking, like typing, are no longer written down as words you said.
- Copy Transcript, next to Copy Notes, copies the Transcript with who said each line and when.
- To remove Bekitzur, drag it to the Trash or choose Delete Bekitzur… in Settings. It offers to move its models (about 18 GB), or all of its data, to the Trash too.
