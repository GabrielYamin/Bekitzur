- Connecting or unplugging headphones during a recording no longer makes Bekitzur quit, or stop recording the other side of the call.
- "2 people at once" in the Transcript now shows only when people talk over each other for at least a second, and holds only what they said at once. A quick "yes" or "right" over someone reads as its own line after theirs.
- When Bekitzur separates people who talk at once, it no longer writes a word twice.
- If part of a recording can't be read, Bekitzur uses the rest and says which part was missing, instead of writing the Notes from the first minutes only.
