# Bekitzur

A Mac app that records Conversations, transcribes them in Hebrew or English, recognizes who spoke, and writes English Notes for the user to review and share. Everything is processed on the user's Mac.

## Language

### Conversations and audio

**Conversation**:
One stretch of talk the app processes as a unit: a Teams call, a Slack huddle, a phone call through the Mac, or an in-person discussion, with or without a calendar invite. Each Conversation has one language, Hebrew or English.
_Avoid_: Meeting, session, discussion

**Recording**:
The captured audio of a Conversation: the microphone and the Mac's system audio. A Conversation outlives its Recording.
_Avoid_: Audio file, capture

**Transcript**:
The verbatim text of a Conversation, attributed to Speakers, with English words kept in English letters.
_Avoid_: Transcription (that's the process)

**Vocabulary**:
The English terms and names the app expects to hear in Conversations: a built-in list of common tech terms plus the team's own terms.
_Avoid_: Dictionary, custom words

### People

**Speaker**:
An anonymous voice within one Conversation, such as "Speaker 2".
_Avoid_: Voice, participant

**Participant**:
A known person the app recognizes across Conversations by their Voiceprint.
_Avoid_: Contact, attendee, user

**Voiceprint**:
The stored voice signature of a Participant, learned when the user names a Speaker.
_Avoid_: Voice profile, embedding

### Notes

**Notes**:
The English write-up of a Conversation: a summary, Decisions, Action Items, and Open Questions, plus optional sections.
_Avoid_: Summary (that's one section), minutes, recap

**Decision**:
A conclusion reached in a Conversation.

**Action Item**:
Something someone committed to in a Conversation: what, its Owner, and a due date if one was said.
_Avoid_: Task, todo, ticket

**Owner**:
Whoever an Action Item is assigned to: a Participant, an unnamed Speaker, or anyone named in the Conversation, even if they weren't there.
_Avoid_: Assignee

**Open Question**:
Something raised in a Conversation and left unresolved.

**Source**:
The moment in the Transcript that a Decision or Action Item came from.
_Avoid_: Citation, evidence

### Review

**Review**:
The user's pass over a processed Conversation to name Speakers and approve or edit its Notes.

**Unreviewed**:
A processed Conversation whose Review isn't done yet.
